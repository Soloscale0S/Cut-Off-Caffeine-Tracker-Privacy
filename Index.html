Privacy Policy for Caffeine Tracker

Last updated: 24 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Caffeine Tracker ("the app", "we") handles your information. Caffeine Tracker is developed and published by Soloscale.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Caffeine Tracker stores all of your information — including your drink logs, caffeine intake history, personal settings, sensitivity preferences, and streak data — locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us, and we do not have access to it. We do not operate any servers that receive or store your personal data, and the app does not require an account or login.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, location, contacts, or any personally identifiable information. The app contains no analytics or advertising tracking that sends your usage data to us.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

If you choose to purchase a premium subscription or a lifetime upgrade, the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing. We use RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service, to manage and verify these purchases. To do this, RevenueCat and Google Play may process purchase history, subscription status, and device identifiers necessary to deliver and validate your purchase. We do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled directly by Google Play.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Notifications

Caffeine Tracker schedules local notifications on your device (for example, alerts about caffeine that may affect your sleep). These notifications are generated and delivered on your device and do not involve sending your data to us or any third party.

5. Children

Caffeine Tracker is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over.

6. Data Deletion

Because all your data is stored locally on your device, you can permanently delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at flowduecom@gmail.com.
